Simple on purpose

Day/night ribbonsOne row per city. Each column is the same instant everywhere.
Time travelDrag, scroll or click an hour to plan across zones. Now snaps back.
Meeting overlapSee at a glance when everyone is in working hours.
Copy the momentPaste a time for every city straight into a chat or invite.
Smart searchCities, airport codes (SFO), abbreviations (PST) and offsets (UTC+5:30).
Menu bar or desktopAn icon or “LON 14:32” in the menu bar, plus a floating desktop window.

Install

  1. Download the latest Meridian.dmg and drag Meridian into Applications.
  2. First launch: right-click Meridian and choose Open, or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Meridian.app
  3. Click the globe in your menu bar, then press + to add cities.
